How I Recycle

This page is for everyone who is looking to up their recycling game, this page will help you get started with the basics

It all starts at the drop off spot, which thankfully people have figured out after doing it a couple of times.
I get a lot of Printers, TVs, Laptops, Microwaves, Tower PCs, almost anything that plugs into a wall.
I take them into the garage, dissassemble them, and sort the differnent metals found within.



Here's a little demo of what you can find in a TV:

This is what the inside of a TV can look like.
They can vary a lot, but they all have 3 circut boards that make them work.
In the picture on the left, you can see two of them, the third is hidden by the metal plate that we use to mount to the wall.

In this picture, you can see the third circut board that was hiding benieth the metal plate on the left. If you look closely, you'll see a bunch of tiny spools with copper inside them. We like those, mainly because copper is very common in electronics and goes for a pretty penny at scrap yards. So we like to take those, because they're worth the effort of taking them off of the spools.
We also like the wires that connect all of the circut boards together, because they also have copper inside them, but the best part, is that the scap yards will just take the wire directly. If you want a bit of extra cash, you can strip the wires to get the bare copper, but I get so many wires that it's just not worth the effor.

The big piece of metal on the green circut board on the left is a piece of aluminum, which we like. aluminum usually goes for a decent amount at the scrap yards.
