Specialized Recycling Information

Some electronics require extra attention because they contain different metals and parts.

Electronics are not just trash. Many items contain copper wire, aluminum, steel, circuit boards, and other recyclable materials. Sue separates these parts so useful materials can be recycled instead of being sent directly to the landfill.

Items like TVs, computers, and printers often need to be taken apart carefully. The valuable parts are not always visible from the outside. After opening the item, Sue can identify wires, circuit boards, small pieces of copper, and larger metal pieces.

Some items should not be handled the same way as normal electronics. Tube TVs and items containing Freon need special disposal methods. If an item has refrigerant, it should be handled through the proper local disposal process.
