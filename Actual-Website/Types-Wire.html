Types of Wire

Different wires can contain different recyclable metals.

Standard Wires:

Standard wires are found in many electronics and appliances. They may look simple, but they can still contain useful recyclable metal inside the plastic coating.

Copper Wire:

Copper wire is one of the most useful types of wire found in electronics. It is often inside cords, appliances, computers, and TVs. Copper is valuable because it can be recycled and reused.

Learn more about copper

Ribbon Wires:

Ribbon wires are flat groups of wires that are often found inside electronics. They are used to connect different parts inside a device, especially in computers, printers, and screens.

Aluminum and Copper Wiring:

Some wiring contains copper, while other wiring may contain aluminum or another metal. Color, weight, and material can help identify what type of wire is being sorted.
