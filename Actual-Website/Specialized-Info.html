Specialized Recycling Information

Some electronics require extra attention because they contain different parts and materials.

Inside a TV:

Televisions can contain circuit boards, wires, metal plates, and other parts that can be separated for recycling. The useful pieces are not always visible from the outside, so the item often needs to be opened carefully.

Hard Drives:

Hard drives need special attention because they may contain personal data. After they are removed from computers, they should be damaged so the data cannot be easily read before recycling.

Power Supply Parts:

Power supplies can contain wires, circuit boards, screws, and metal pieces. These parts can be separated and sorted, but they should be handled carefully because they may have sharp edges or small components.
