Recycling Information

Why Recycle?🠟

Recycling is easy to do, and has a big enviromental impact. According to the EPA, recycling one ton of office papers saves the same amount of energy as would be used by consuming 322 gallons of gasoline, and recycling just ten plastic bottles saves enough energy to power a laptop for over 25 hours.


What can be Recycled?🠟

Some of the most common recycled items are cardboard, paper, food boxes, mail, cans, bottles, jars, and jugs. Many items that are plugged into a wall also have some parts that can be recycled. If you are unsure of what can be recycled, check in with your local recycling provider.


Why Did Sue Start Her Recycling Business?🠟

Sue first started learning about recycling when she inherited a number of ugly brass figurines. She took them to be recycled, and ended up talking to a lot of different people about how to recycle, and learned from them. She started out her recycling business, by driving around during garbage day in different areas to look for recyclables that people were throwing away, which evolved into posting an advertisement online to invite people to give her their recyclables.
