Reducing Your Carbon Footprint

Here are some steps that I take to reduce my carbon footprint:

  • Consider solar energy
  • electric/battery operated yard tools/machines
  • Recycle
  • Turn off lights when leaving rooms
  • Unplug appliances not in use
  • Don't put recyclables in plastic bags
  • Try composting your food scraps
  • Minimize good packaging - use natural glass containers when possible
  • Use your own bags when grocery shopping
  • Reuse reuse reuse reuse
  • Donate stuff you don't use
  • Share with friends - not everyone has to have their own chainsaw, etc.
