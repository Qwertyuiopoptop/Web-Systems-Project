Identifying Different Metals


Aluminum Silver in color, non-magnetic, and lighter than it looks.
Clean Copper Copper colored, non-magnetic, shiny, clean, and bare.
Dirty Copper Identifiable as copper (copper colored, non-magentic), but with anything else on it, such as solder, stickers, plastic, or oxidation.
Insulated Copper Wiring and extension cords, or any other kind of cord/wire.
Brass Non-magnetic and solid yellow in color.
Stainless Steel Silver colored, non-magnetic, will be heavier than aluminum.
Gold or Silver Can be found in computer componenets such as the RAM or Processor.
