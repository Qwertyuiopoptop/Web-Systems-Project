Where to take Accepted Items

Take your recyclable items to 39 South Locust Street, Oconomowoc WI, 53066

Drop Off Instructions

There will be a recycling logo on the right side of my garage door, and underneath will be a recycling bin. Please put anything that you have in the bin, and if the bin is full, start a pile off to the right of the bin. You can drop items off at any time, whether I'm home or not.
