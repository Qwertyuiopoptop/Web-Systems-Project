Tools I Use

These are some of the tools Sue uses to safely take apart old electronics.

Basic Hand Tools:

Tools! Screw drivers, wire cutters, various hex drivers to aid in breaking down the electronics.
I use these most often, that is why they are kept on the tool magnet holder.

Screwdriver Kit:

A big set of the different types of screw bits - all different sizes and shapes. Star shape, square shape, Y shape - super helpful in taking apart laptops. Different manufacturers use different shapes. This set has a quick change snap onto a handle and some prybars. It even has a magnet to help pick up small screws.

Magnets and Special Screws:

Magnet on a key-chain - a recycler’s friend. Used to pick up small screws, and also to determine type of metal. Steel is magnetic, brass, copper, aluminum and stainless steel are not magnetic. So if the magnet sticks it is steel (the lowest priced metal). If you have two different metals attached to each other and one is magnetic and the other isn’t it may be worth your time to separate them.
