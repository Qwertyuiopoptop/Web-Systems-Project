Specialized Recycling Information

Some electronics require extra attention because they contain different parts and materials.

Inside a TV:

Flat screen TV with the back off - notice the various wires and the green circuit board. All recyclable - along with the metal that is the skeleton of the TV, which is usually steel. However sometimes there is aluminum hidden so keep an eye out for it.

Hard Drives:

Computer tower hard drives - once you take them apart you can recycle the metal. And to render the data on the hard drive unreadable I usually scratch the disk inside and bend and if possible - they are usually aluminum or stainless steel. Laptop drive are much smaller and can be easily broken into many pieces. (I crush with my hand!)

Power Supply Parts:

Another look at a power supply - once I get the wires, aluminum, and copper off I usually toss this in with the steel
