Tools I Use

These are some of the tools Sue uses to safely take apart old electronics.

Basic Hand Tools:

Basic hand tools are important for opening old electronics and separating recyclable materials. Pliers, cutters, and screwdrivers help remove small parts, wires, and screws safely.

Screwdriver Kit:

A screwdriver kit is useful because electronics often use many different screw types. Having different screwdriver heads makes it easier to open devices without damaging the case or parts inside.

Magnets and Special Screws:

A magnet can help identify whether a piece of metal is steel or another type of metal. Star screwdrivers are also useful because many electronics use screws that normal screwdrivers cannot remove.

Learn more about hand tool safety

These tools make the recycling process easier and more organized. After electronics are opened, the materials can be sorted into groups like copper wire, aluminum, steel, plastic, and circuit boards.
