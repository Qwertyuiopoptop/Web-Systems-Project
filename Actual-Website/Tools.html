Tools I Use

These are some of the tools Sue uses to safely take apart old electronics.

Sue uses basic hand tools to open electronics and separate recyclable materials. Some common tools include screwdrivers, wire cutters, pliers, safety gloves, and socket wrenches. These tools help her remove screws, cut wires, and separate metal, plastic, and other recyclable parts.

Safety is important when taking apart electronics. Gloves help protect hands from sharp edges, broken plastic, and metal pieces. Wire cutters and pliers are useful for removing cords and smaller parts without damaging the recyclable material too much.

Using the right tools makes recycling easier and more organized. After the items are taken apart, Sue can sort the materials into different groups, such as copper wire, aluminum, steel, and circuit boards.
